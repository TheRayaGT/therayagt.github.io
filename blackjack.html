    House rules

    Each table deals from its own six-deck shoe, reshuffled when the cut card comes out (and mid-hand if a shoe ever runs dry).

    Dealer stands on soft 17 and checks for blackjack with an ace or ten showing. Blackjack pays 3 to 2, insurance pays 2 to 1.

    Double on any first two cards. Split pairs up to four hands; split aces get one card each. Late surrender returns half your bet.

    No table limit: bet anything up to what's in your bank. Keys act on the highlighted table: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, R surrender, I/N insurance, Enter deal all.
