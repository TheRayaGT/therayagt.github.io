Creatives

I am interested in landscape photography, long exposures and timelapse films.

Films

I am inspired by the timelapse photographers Dimid Vazhnik, Kirill Neiezhmakov and Keith Loutit, and by the flowmotion photographer Rob Whitworth.

Fieldwork

I work mainly in landscape photography, and I have shot in Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur and Dubai. As of August 2026, I have also photographed in the Chinese cities below, with more to come. I like long exposures on a tripod, and I hope to shoot star trails some day.

I would be happy to share some nice itineraries.
